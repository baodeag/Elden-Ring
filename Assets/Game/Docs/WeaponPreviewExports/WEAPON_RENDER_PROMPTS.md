# Weapon Render Prompts

Ban prompt nay da duoc siet lai de uu tien giong prefab toi da, bao gom ca block mau theo tung phan.

## Muc Tieu

- low poly toi gian nhat co the
- giong silhouette prefab toi da
- giong block mau prefab toi da
- tranh AI tu sang tac them chi tiet hoac doi palette
- icon PNG nen trong suot
- weapon dat cheo trong khung

## Cach Dung Tot Nhat

1. Attach dung preview PNG cua weapon can render.
2. Paste prompt tu CSV/JSON.
3. Neu mau van lech, nhac them: `match the reference color blocking exactly, do not invent a new palette`.
4. Neu model van bi lech, nhac them: `match the reference silhouette even more strictly`.
5. Neu AI van ve qua chi tiet, nhac them: `reduce detail further, keep large flat low-poly planes only`.

## Luu Y

- Prompt da them color-placement constraints theo tung nhom weapon.
- Prompt da uu tien exact silhouette, exact proportions, va exact color blocking hon la dep fantasy.
